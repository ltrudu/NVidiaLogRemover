🐘 What is the problem?

Your NVIDIA graphics card (the part that draws the pictures of your games) has a small program that writes a log: it writes down everything that happens, like a diary.

With two versions of the driver, 621.14 and 621.24, this log goes crazy: it copies everything again, and again, and again. It can grow by 30 GB per hour of gaming! It's as if someone rewrote a whole notebook every time they added one line. 📓📓📓

💡 The repair program empties this giant log, removes the wrong driver and installs a good driver, version 621.01.

🚀 Start the program

  1. Open the program's folder and double-click ⚙️ RUN_ME_English.bat
  2. Windows asks: "Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your device?" Click Yes. If a password is requested, call an adult.
  3. The "NVIDIA Repair" window opens. 🎉 It immediately looks at the state of your computer.
⚠️ Don't move or rename the scripts folder: it must stay next to RUN_ME_English.bat. That's where the program keeps its tools!

🟡 Follow the yellow button!

The program is a detective 🕵️: every time it opens, and after every button you click, it checks where you are. Is the log emptied? Is the wrong driver gone? Is the right driver downloaded? Installed?

Then it turns the button for the next step yellow, and the blue sentence tells you what to do. You click the yellow button, read the messages, and click OK when asked. Repeat until you see "Everything is fixed!" 🎉

🔄 If you need to restart

  1. The program tells you to restart. The "Restart the computer" button turns yellow: click it.
  2. When the computer is back on, run ⚙️ RUN_ME_English.bat again and click Yes.
  3. The program checks where you are and turns the next step yellow. It forgets nothing, even after a restart! 🧠

📦 While the driver installs

An NVIDIA window opens: click OK, accept, then Next each time, and Close at the end.
🖥️ The screen may go black for a few seconds: that's normal, don't touch anything!
⏳ The download is about 1 GB. A progress bar shows how far it is. It can take several minutes: go get a glass of water! 🥤

🛡️ Automatic cleanup (the shield)

You can turn on a shield that watches the log by itself, even when the program is closed. If the log is larger than 200 MB, it is deleted automatically. 🧹

At the bottom of the window, you choose when the shield works:

🚪

When Windows starts and shuts down

✅ Recommended

The shield checks the log when the computer turns on, when you log in, and when you shut down or restart the computer.

👍 It never works during a game: your games are safe.

⏰

Every 3 hours

⚠️ Careful

The shield checks at start-up, then every 3 hours, even while you are playing.

😬 To empty the log, it has to stop the NVIDIA service for a moment. If that happens during a game, the game can freeze, flicker or crash. The program will ask you to confirm this choice.

🤔 Which one should I pick? Choose "When Windows starts and shuts down". The "every 3 hours" mode is only useful if the computer stays on for days without ever being shut down.

🧪 Try it: pick a mode and look at your day

🆘 If you get stuck

An "Oops, a problem" message appears

Don't panic! Leave the window open and call an adult. Show them the message and the list of actions at the bottom of the window.

I clicked "No" instead of "Yes"

No problem: run RUN_ME_English.bat again and click Yes this time.

The download is very long or stuck

Check that the computer is connected to the Internet (Wi-Fi). If the download stops, the program shows a message. Then click "3. Download the right driver" again.

The screen went black!

While the driver installs, that's normal: the screen may flicker or go black for a few seconds. Wait without touching anything. ⏳

After the restart, the wrong driver came back

Windows may have reinstalled it by itself. Run the program again and follow the yellow button: it will have you install the right driver on top of it.

The program says "Everything is fixed!"

Well done, mission accomplished! 🎉 You can close the window. Remember to turn on the automatic cleanup ("When Windows starts and shuts down") if you haven't yet.

I want to close the window while the program is working

The program asks if you are sure. It's better to wait until it's finished, especially during an uninstall or an install.
